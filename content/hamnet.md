## Overview

Hamnet (2025) is Chloe Zhao's adaptation of Maggie O'Farrell's novel, starring Jessie Buckley as Agnes and Paul Mescal as William Shakespeare. The film reimagines the real-life death of Shakespeare's 11-year-old son Hamnet and how that loss became the raw material for Hamlet.

## Key Themes

- **Grief as creation** — The film's central tension: Shakespeare channels unbearable personal loss into art, raising the question of whether great work requires great suffering
- **The invisible labor of women** — Agnes holds the family together while Will builds his legacy in London. The story belongs to her as much as to him
- **Presence vs. absence** — Shakespeare is physically absent for his son's death. The film sits in that gap between the life he chose and the life that continued without him

## Why It Matters

Zhao does something rare here — she makes a period drama feel immediate and visceral rather than museum-piece polished. The Golden Globe win for Best Drama was earned. A 10/10 that stays with you.
